1. Deep Project & Usage Analysis

A comprehensive evaluation of DroidTree's core engine, usage type, and user persona to dictate an enduring visual identity.

⚡ The Usage Type: High-Speed Disk Visualizer

Unlike passive utility tools, DroidTree is an active, exploratory instrument. Users launch it when storage is tight and need immediate, visceral answers. The logo must embody speed, depth, and clarity rather than generic "cleaning brooms" or static wrenches.

🗂️ The Mathematical Core: Squarified Treemap

The heart of DroidTree is its TreemapCanvas: recursive geometric division where area directly maps to byte size. Incorporating this golden-ratio rectangular partition into the mark anchors the visual identity directly to the app's real computational magic.

🍎 Design Language: Apple HIG & Fluid Craft

Built with Compose spring physics (SpringSpecs), fluid touch surfaces (FluidCard), and circular gauges (StorageGaugeRing). The identity applies Apple's continuous curvature superellipse (squircle), optical stroke weighting, and translucent glass layers.
